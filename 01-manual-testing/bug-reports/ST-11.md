# ST-11: [Checkout] Back button reopens a $0.00 checkout

| Severity | Priority | Test case | Account | Environment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Medium | CHK-17 | standard_user | Windows 11 · Chrome 155 · 1920×1080 |

## Preconditions
1. Logged in as standard_user
2. Sauce Labs Backpack in the cart
3. On Inventory page

## Steps to reproduce

1. Click the cart icon.
2. Click Checkout.
3. Enter Jane / Doe / 12345.
4. Click Continue.
5. Click Finish.
6. On the "Thank you for your order!" page, press the browser Back button.

## Expected result
After clicking Finish, the user should not be able to return to Checkout: Overview. If they go back, they should be redirected away from the completed checkout.

## Actual result
After the order is completed, pressing the browser Back button reopens Checkout: Overview with an empty cart and a $0.00 total. The order can then be finished again.

## Evidence

![CHK-17 Back button after completed order](../evidence/CHK-17_Back_button_after_completed_order.png)

## Impact
A customer can place a second, empty $0.00 order just by pressing Back after a purchase. Like [ST-4](ST-4.md), this lets orders with no items reach order records and fulfillment.

## Suggested action
Once an order is completed, treat the checkout as closed: redirect any return to Checkout: Overview to the Cart or Inventory page. Also reject Finish when the cart is empty, which fixes ST-4 as well.
